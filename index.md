# Premiere tools to make life easier.

## Syncpoint

[Try free](https://syncpoint.video/download)

Sync your whole Premiere project with one click.

Every camera and recorder, lined up by timecode and audio in a new sequence. No XML, nothing to relink.

$99 · Mac and Windows

## Pullquote

[Download free](https://figure5media.com/downloads/Pullquote.ccx)

Paste the script. Get the cut.

Finds every scripted line in your interviews and builds the soundbites into a new sequence, in script order.

Free · Mac and Windows

**Inside Premiere** Each tool is a panel in your workspace. No round trips, no XML, no second app.

**Your sequence stays yours** Both build a new sequence beside the original. Nothing you made is changed.

**No subscriptions** Syncpoint is $99, once. Pullquote is free.
