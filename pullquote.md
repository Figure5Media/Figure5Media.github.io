## Install

About a minute. You need Premiere 26.0 or later and the Creative Cloud app.

1. **Open the file.** Double-click Pullquote.ccx. Creative Cloud asks whether to install a plugin from outside its store: click Install.
2. **Open the panel.** In Premiere, choose Window > UXP Plugins > Pullquote. If it isn't there yet, restart Premiere.
3. **Paste a script.** Accept the license the first time it opens, then paste your script and press Find Soundbites.

The panel tells you when there's a new version: download it and open it the same way. To remove Pullquote, use Manage Plugins in the Creative Cloud app.

## Questions

### Does Pullquote change my sequence or my media?
No. It reads the sequence in front of you and builds a new one beside it, named with "Script 1" at the end. Your original timeline and every media file stay exactly as they were.

### Doesn't Premiere's Paper Edit do this?
Paper Edit builds a cut from passages you find and highlight in the transcript yourself. Pullquote starts from the script instead: it finds each line for you, shows the other takes when a line was said more than once, and builds the whole sequence in one press.

### Does it transcribe my footage?
No. It reads the transcripts Premiere makes in its Text panel. Clips without a transcript are skipped and named, so you know which ones to transcribe.

### Is my script or footage sent anywhere?
No. Everything runs on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account.

### What if a line in the script was never said?
It stays plain in the panel, outside the green and amber blocks, and is left out of the new sequence. The line under the sequence's name counts how many were found, how many to check, and how many weren't found.

### What if the speaker said a line more than once?
Pullquote picks one and shows the other takes under it. Click through them and choose the one you want before you build, or duplicate the block to keep two takes.

### What can't it do yet?
Clips with a speed change aren't read yet. Your sequence's own markers don't carry into the new one.

### Does it work on Windows?

Yes. Pullquote runs the same on Mac and Windows.
