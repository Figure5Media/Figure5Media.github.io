# Guides

Short, practical guides to cutting interviews to a script in Premiere.

- [How to cut an interview to a client's script in Premiere](https://figure5media.com/cut-interview-to-script-premiere): The client sends a script of the lines they want. Three ways to find those lines in your interview footage and build the cut in Premiere, from slowest to fastest.
- [How to pull selects from a transcript in Premiere](https://figure5media.com/pull-selects-from-transcript-premiere): How to turn a transcript into a sequence of selects in Premiere: with text-based editing, with Paper Edit, and from a written list of lines with Pullquote.
- [Pullquote and Premiere's Paper Edit: what each one does](https://figure5media.com/pullquote-vs-paper-edit): Premiere's Paper Edit builds a sequence from transcript passages you select. Pullquote finds a written script's lines for you and builds the sequence. When to use which.
